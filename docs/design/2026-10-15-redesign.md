# Site Search redesign (Oct 2026)

Figma: https://www.figma.com/design/pSYn1a7EtoDH2bkonZ7Nfj
Inspiration: Intercom web on Mobbin (inbox, contacts, reports, settings home).

## What was wrong

| Page | Problem |
|---|---|
| Today | A flat list of cards. Nothing tells you how the business is doing, what is running, or what to do first. |
| All leads | Works, but three different 0–100 numbers sit side by side with no explanation, and every column competes for color. |
| Lead detail | One long column. The scores have no meaning attached, issues use seven category colors plus three severity colors, and the tabs hide the email draft and pipeline. |
| New search | 150 checkboxes in 11 groups, one long scroll, and the cost summary is at the very bottom. |
| Settings | One page with ~60 fields, 4 editors and 2 side cards. Hard to find anything. |
| Navigation | 7 top links with equal weight. Promising, Radar and Import are really views of Leads and Searches. |

## New information architecture

Icon rail (always visible) + a contextual sidebar per section, content on a floating white panel.

- **Home** (was Today): action queue, KPIs, pipeline, spend, running searches in the sidebar.
- **Leads**: views (All, Starred, Needs full scan (was Promising), Drafts ready), stages (New → Lost), saved filters, Import, Archived.
- **Find**: New search, Search history, Radar (recurring), Import.
- **Outreach**: drafts queue (every unsent draft in one place).
- **Settings**: a home of category cards, each opening a short focused page.

## Score system (the main readability fix)

Only one number gets color: the **lead score** (was "Opportunity").

| Score | Shown as | Meaning shown in UI |
|---|---|---|
| Lead score (Opportunity) | Orange / amber / grey pill: Hot 60+, Warm 25–59, Cool <25 | "How strong a prospect is. Higher is better." |
| Site health | Neutral ink bar + number + Poor/Fair/Good | "How good their site is now. Low = more to fix." |
| Fit | Plain mono number | "Match with your fit profiles." |
| Urgency | Lead detail only | "How much time pressure the findings add up to." |

The lead page breaks the lead score into its parts: weak website (+56), critical gaps (+15), established business (+15).

## Issues

- Severity uses a 3-bar signal icon + word (Critical / Important / Nice to have). Only Critical is red, Important is amber, Nice is grey.
- Categories use icons, not colors (target = Conversion, code = Technical & SEO, phone = Mobile, gauge = Speed, document = Content, layout = Design).
- Issues are grouped into one card per category with its score, sorted worst first.

## What's in the Figma file

- **Cover**
- **Foundations**: brand, principles, color tokens, type scale, score system.
- **Components**: 58 icons, Button, Badge, Nav item, Lead score (Hot/Warm/Cool), Severity, Checkbox, Toggle, Avatar, Input.
- **Screens** (30, in four labeled rows):
  - Core app: Home, All leads, Lead · Overview, Lead · Website issues, Lead · Email draft, New search, Settings home, Settings · Profile.
  - Find, Outreach, more lead tabs: Search history, Search progress, Radar, Import (review step), Outreach drafts inbox, Lead · Conversion plan, Lead · Activity.
  - Settings pages: Job search, Email signature, Email voice, Work samples, Services you sell, Fit profiles, Suppressions, Google, Usage & limits, Mailing footer, How scores work.
  - Public report (desktop and phone), Login (default and error).

New in the IA, not in the app today: the **Outreach** inbox (all unsent drafts in one queue), the **How scores work** guide, and **Mailing footer** as its own Settings page. Promising becomes the "Needs full scan" view under Leads.

Not designed: empty states, the Import paste/upload step and its "finished" step, CRO audit running/failed states, and the evidence drawer.
