# Plan 8 (Stripe billing: invoices, subscriptions, reminders and the pause): open items after the build

Branch `feat/agency-os-billing`, built 2026-11-20 by subagents (all on Sonnet). One migration, `0057_billing_invoices` (it rebuilds `invoices` and adds the holds, subscriptions, reminder-nudge and billing-settings storage; `0053` is untouched). Nothing is deployed, pushed, merged or migrated remotely, and nothing has been run against Stripe, not even in test mode.

What was built: invoices are now drafted by the app and become Stripe invoices only when you approve them. The milestones of a signed proposal bill themselves: when a project reaches `design_approved` or `site_finished`, the amount the client signed is drafted as an invoice (from the frozen schedule, never today's catalog) and put up as an `invoice.send` approval; approving it makes the Stripe invoice (one idempotency key per invoice, so a retry replays instead of duplicating) and mails Stripe's link. The deposit stays as Plan 5 built it, made on the client's page. A change order's invoice is drafted when the client signs, an early-exit balance when a subscription is cancelled early, and finished billable time can be invoiced by the hour (30-minute blocks, the Care rate for a Care client). The Stripe webhook now handles every kind (`invoice.paid`, `.payment_failed`, `.voided`, `.marked_uncollectible`, `checkout.session.completed`, `customer.subscription.updated` and `.deleted`): only the amount Stripe reports as paid, in `usd`, against the invoice's own `ref`, marks an invoice paid, anything else records a mismatch; a Stripe-paid change order unlocks its tasks once. Recurring money is a subscription row planned when the deposit is paid (Care, Website Subscription, Local SEO), proposed when its trigger happens (signing, the build finishing, or your "Start billing" at kickoff), approved by you, and paid by the client on a stable page `<CLIENT_LINK_BASE>/s/<token>` that makes a fresh Stripe Checkout (card only, hosted by Stripe) on each click. The Website Subscription tracks its 24-month term, drafts a notice 30 days before it ends, and ends or converts at the term; leaving early shows the balance and needs your confirmation. A reminder ladder (default 3 days before the due date, on it, 3 and 7 days after) drafts one fixed-template reminder per step as an approval, and 14 days after the due date a per-client hold stops your and Claude's tasks (not the client's site, hosting or Care monitoring) and drafts one pause notice, also an approval; payment, voiding or you lifts it, and the waiting Claude steps are sent in the same moment. The daily cron now ends with `sweepBilling` (drafts the milestone invoices a hook never made, plans and proposes subscriptions, drafts reminders and notices, places holds, notices and ends terms, and settles open invoices whose webhook never came), and it only creates approvals and holds, never sends. The owner side has a Billing page, a Billing card and tab on each project, Settings > Billing (the invoice terms and reminder settings, the Stripe status and the seven webhook events to add) and Today rows; the client side is the `/s/<token>` page (`/api/public/s/<token>` and `/checkout`).

One end-to-end test (`test/billing-flow.test.ts`) drives the real entry points (the signed Stripe webhook, the owner's routes under `/api`, the approvals route, the client's public `/s/` and `/c/` pages, the MCP tool `propose_approval`, the daily cron) against fakes for Stripe, the mail transport and the PDF browser, on a fixed calendar (Los Angeles, from 1 Dec 2026 to 16 Feb 2029). It tells one story in order: a signed Growth proposal with Care is paid by webhook; the 30% at design approved is drafted, approved, invoiced, mailed and paid twice by webhook with one `invoice_paid`; the last 30% is sent and left unpaid while the cron walks 18 days of the ladder (four reminders, each drafted once and approved) and places the hold at day 14 with one pause notice; while held, a Claude step is not dispatched, Ask Claude is refused and a finished Care project makes no recurring task; the paid webhook lifts the hold and queues the waiting step in the same request; the Care subscription is approved, opened by the client, paid at Checkout and activated by webhook, then a month fails (past due) and succeeds (active); a signed change order drafts its invoice, is approved, invoiced and paid by webhook, and unlocks its task once; one Website Subscription ends at month 24 and another is cancelled early and owes the balance. It found no defect in the code it exercises (see Known gaps for what it showed). Every page was only checked by typecheck, build and stubbed or static browser harnesses, never against the real Worker, real Stripe, or a real browser.

The deposit stays as Plan 5 built it (made on the client's page). Reminder mails and the pause notice are approvals of fixed templates, not Claude-drafted (spec §10 lists them as templates; spec §9.7 says Claude drafts the pause notice, which was replaced by the template to avoid a queue job for a form letter).
2. One due date, one ladder. Offsets from the due date default to -3, 0, +3 and +7 days (`reminderOffsets`), the hold at +14 (`pauseAfterDays`), net terms 7 days (`netDays`), all in Settings > Billing. While held: no Claude dispatch, no recurring tasks, no step results applied, no new project starts work. You can still work tasks by hand. The client's site, hosting and Care monitoring are untouched.

Monthly only: annual billing is not built (the proposal schedule has no annual price).

Launch: when the build project finishes, first charge that day (a first charge less than 3 days out is taken at Checkout; Stripe needs 48 hours for a trial). ## Known gaps (decided, not fixed)

Invoice model and storage
- `holdFreeSql` uses an inner alias `h` that collides if a caller passes `"h"` as its own alias (the one caller passes `"p"`).
- `parseLines` coerces a NaN amount; `insertInvoice` defaults `test` to false (only the deposit calls it); the invoice amount and the sum of its lines are not schema-enforced.
- No index on `invoices.business_id` (Today runs an `EXISTS` per business).

Stripe client
- `createSubscriptionCheckout` does not itself check that `successUrl` and `cancelUrl` are https (the page's Checkout start does, from `CLIENT_LINK_BASE`).
- `voidInvoice`'s key `void:<invoiceId>` replays a first failure for about 24 hours.
- The `trial_end` threshold is measured from a noon-UTC timestamp against the clock, not by date arithmetic, so a same-day replay across the 3-day line changes the request body under one idempotency key.
- The fake Stripe's subscription POST models only `cancel_at_period_end`, not a price change on re-read.

Draft, send and milestones
- Untested branches: a draft discarded while Stripe is working (the void path), the do-not-contact block, and the booking and lead recipient fallbacks. Nine minors from this task's review are parked in the ledger.
- `voidSentInvoice` after the 24-hour idempotency window on an invoice already void at Stripe would error rather than count as success.
- A race test for the hourly claim would pass without the count and sum guards; `hourlyInvoice` returns null after three lost claims (reads as "nothing to bill"); the Care-rate test covers `care` and not `care_plus`.

Webhook and payments
- Checkout activation fails open when no Checkout session id is stored (the page always stores one before the link goes out; consider failing closed for `link_sent` with a null id).
- A test-mode session (`livemode` false) is not rejected for a live subscription, and a test-mode event naming a live row is not rejected; the metadata fallback attaches an invoice regardless of the subscription's status.
- `past_due` returns to `active` when any month is paid, even if another month's invoice is still failed.
- `test/stripe-webhook.test.ts` was not extended (`test/billing-paid.test.ts` covers the new events); the hold-lift test does not assert that `dispatchReady` ran.
- A cancelled subscription's `cancelled_at` is the time of the cancel request, not the end of the paid period (it means "will not renew"), so MRR drops at the request.

Subscriptions and Checkout
- A first charge date in the past is not guarded: Checkout charges at once (the link mail now says the first payment is taken when they set it up).
- A midnight-straddling first click can make two Checkout sessions (the latest stored wins; the store is not guarded on "no session yet"); `let stored` is shadowed in `checkout.ts`.
- A cancelled or ended subscription's link answers 200 with state `closed` on GET and 409 on POST, not 404 as the global constraint says (an unknown, malformed or not-yet-sent link does answer 404); the client page's copy depends on it.
- No handler for `async_payment_succeeded` or `async_payment_failed`; subscription Checkout is card only so none is needed today, see Decisions 14. "Subscription link sent" uses the existing `note` activity kind.

Term, conversion and early exit
- The subscription is read at the start of the term sweep and not again before the Stripe call.
- If Stripe's cancel succeeds but the guarded row update changes nothing (you flip the end action at that moment), the next sweep replays under the same key.
- `cancelSubscription` recomputes the balance from "now" with no pinned amount, so the confirmed figure and the invoiced one can differ across a month boundary.
- A rejected or never-actioned term notice does not block conversion. `term_ends_on` is set to NULL on a conversion to Care (intended).
- Untested: `termNotice.execute` for a test subscription, a Stripe-ok-then-draft-fail early-exit retry, concurrent double cancel.

Reminders and the hold
- No test for the claim rollback when `createApproval` throws; a hard crash between the nudge insert and the approval loses that step (the next step still goes).
- The "two sweeps at one moment" test uses `Promise.all`, which may be serialized.
- A non-subscription invoice that reaches `failed` with a `failed_on` moves the ladder's base (only delays it).
- The pause notice's "N days overdue" can differ between the preview and the send (it is accurate at send). Other `enqueueTask` callers (lead and deal work) are outside the hold by design.
- `billing/lift.ts` dispatches even when it lifted zero holds.

Sweep and routes
- Settling now runs first in `sweepBilling`; it costs one Stripe GET per stale open invoice per day.
- `routes/billing.ts` maps any bare `Error` to a 409 sentence (a typed refusal class would be tighter). Partly planned documents are not retried.

Owner UI
- The Today "Invoice drafted: needs your OK" row also shows for a draft that already has a pending `invoice.send` approval (a duplicate nag).
- "Done" on a billing row hides that client's billing row for 7 days, including an escalation to "Work on hold"; the lift confirm text can say "$0" when the invoice is missing from the list; `useNames` refetch is O(n squared); "Sent ..." uses the browser's local date and `useOwnerClock` falls back to the default zone if `/settings` fails; the month-end `subscriptionLine` test only echoes dates.
- No page was opened in a browser; click flows were only read-checked.

Client page
- The pay button is never shown on a stale `?done=1` with state `ready` (stuck at "Check again"; consider showing it after a second stall); the busy-state double-click guard is not airtight (a `useRef` would be); `isCheckoutUrl` blocks a custom Stripe Checkout domain; the "no token or Stripe id in the copy" test is near vacuous.
- The page and the click use separate clocks: a load just over 3 days from the first charge, then a click just under, reads "first charge on <date>" yet charges at Checkout (narrow, accepted).
- The Stripe redirect and the polling loop after Checkout were never tried.

Added in the final review (known, not fixed)
- A voided or discarded milestone or change-order invoice cannot be re-issued through the app: the `ref` stays on the void row. Re-issue it by hand in Stripe, or add a `<ref>:rN` owner action later.
- `due_on` from Stripe is a UTC calendar date while everything else uses the booking zone: a late-evening finalize shows the next day, and reminders and the hold run a day late.
- The `stripe_products` table is created by migration 0057 but unused.
- Stripe idempotency body against Settings changes: changing net days or the footer between a half-finished attempt and its retry inside 24 hours makes Stripe reject the retry.
- `soldAndPaid` on the project card counts the prepaid first month of a recurring line in paid but not in sold.
- `proposeInvoice` does not see an approval that is mid-execution, so a second pending approval is possible (the Stripe invoice stays single).
- The five billing approvals repeat the same mailbox, recipient and conflict gate; consolidate it into one helper later.
- There are five variants of the secret-scrubbing helper and some unscrubbed `console.error` messages (safe today).
- Dead exports: `paidTotalForDeal`, `hourlyRef`, `BLOCK_MINUTES`, `NO_RECIPIENT`.
- `useNames` calls the heavy `GET /leads/:id`.
- The early-exit balance can be one month off when the Checkout anchor differs from `firstChargeOn`.
- The daily sweep dispatches projects before billing places holds (a stale job is guarded).
- The pause notice's lead still names the invoice's due date as the day a failed payment "did not go through" when a failed invoice has no `failed_on` (only the reminder was corrected).
